Privacy Policy

Miti Bar: Nepali Date Status

Package ID: np.com.siriri.mitibar • A product of Siriri Creative • Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Miti Bar: Nepali Date Status is an offline Nepali (Bikram Sambat) date utility that displays the current Nepali date in your Android status bar and lockscreen. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work without collecting any user data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The app requires no account or sign-in, contains no advertisements, and includes no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

2. Network Access & App Permissions

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and never transmits data over any network. It only uses the following Android permissions locally on your device:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display today's Nepali date in your status bar and lockscreen.
  • Midnight Refresh & Reboot Restore (USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to update the displayed Nepali date at midnight and restore it after a device reboot, time change, or timezone change.

3. Local Storage

Your in-app preferences—specifically whether the status bar date is enabled and whether Nepal Time (Asia/Kathmandu) or Local Phone Time is selected—are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and never leave your phone. Backup via Android Auto Backup is also disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses zero third-party SDKs, ad networks, or external APIs.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it complies with applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA).

If future versions of the app introduce features that affect data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated at this URL with a revised effective date.
